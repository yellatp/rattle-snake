# Senior Backend Engineer — FinTech Payments Platform

We are building a low-latency payments processing platform. You will own services
that move money between merchants and banks in real time.

## Requirements
- 5+ years building backend systems in TypeScript, Go, or Java
- Strong systems design: distributed systems, microservices, concurrency, idempotency
- Experience with event-driven architecture (Kafka, SQS, or similar)
- Databases: PostgreSQL at scale, including indexing strategy and partitioning
- PCI-DSS awareness and security-first mindset
- Production debugging, on-call ownership, SLO maintenance

## Nice to have
- Payment domain experience (ledgers, double-entry, settlement)
- Experience with streaming at high throughput
- Familiarity with double-entry accounting systems

## Responsibilities
- Design and ship low-latency payment APIs
- Improve idempotency and retry semantics for payment processing
- Drive architectural decisions around data consistency
- Participate in on-call rotation and production incident response
